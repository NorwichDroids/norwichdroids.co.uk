We build the droids Star Wars only dreamed of.

Norwich Droids is a Norfolk-based crew of makers turning spare parts and stubbornness into screen-accurate astromechs — R2 units, BB-series builds and more — built to turn heads at conventions, raise smiles at charity days, and satisfy that itch only a finished build can scratch.
