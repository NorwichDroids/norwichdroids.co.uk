About Norwich Droids

Norwich Droids is a community of builders across Norfolk, ranging from first-timers with a 3D printer to seasoned fabricators who've been at this for years. Between us we've built R2 units, BB-series droids, and a growing cast of other astromechs — sharing techniques, parts lists, and a lot of tea at build nights.

We meet regularly to build, help newcomers get started, and take our droids out to conventions and charity events across the region. Whatever stage you're at, there's a place for you here.
