Charity & Community

Building droids is a lot of fun on its own, but we like to put that fun to good use too. Outside of conventions and build nights, Norwich Droids regularly brings our astromechs along to local charity fun days, fundraisers, and community events across Norfolk — because a working droid rolling up to say hello raises a smile (and hopefully a few pounds) for causes that matter.

We're proud to support charities that are close to us and close to home. Below are some of the local organisations we back — if you'd like our droids at your charity event, or want to tell us about a cause we should know about, get in touch.

Charities We Support

A few of the local charities and community causes close to our members' hearts.

Norfolk & Waveney Autism/ADHD Support Service

Run by the national charity Family Action, this local service supports autistic people, people with ADHD, and their families across Norfolk and Waveney.
